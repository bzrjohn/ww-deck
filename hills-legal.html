Why Hunter Legal dominates today

  • LegalService schema on homepage with full NAP, service areas, opening hours.

Translation. Hunter has every signal Google actually ranks for in local legal services: schema, reviews, speed, map pack. Hills has none of them converting. Pillar 1 — Technical

Fix the foundation first.

Hills' site loads 212 KB of HTML with 5 render-blocking stylesheets and 6 render-blocking scripts in the head. Hunter ships 99 KB. This is the single biggest reason Hunter gets the rich-snippet treatment on SERPs where Hills has the authority to win. Weeks 1–3.

Schema implementation

  • LegalService — Maitland office, services offered, service areas
  • LocalBusiness / Attorney — NAP, hours, geo coordinates
  • AggregateRating + Review — pipe Google reviews into rich snippets
  • FAQPage — on every service page, targeted at People Also Ask
  • BreadcrumbList — consistent site-wide
  • Person schema — named solicitors, credentials, bar admission
  • Service schema — conveyancing, family law, wills, business

Performance fixes

  • Inline critical CSS, defer non-critical (removes 5 render-blockers)
  • Move 6 head scripts to defer/async or footer
  • Convert all JPG/PNG to WebP with fallback
  • Lazy-load below-fold imagery
  • Preconnect to fonts + GA
  • HTTP/2 + Brotli compression audit
  • Target: mobile Core Web Vitals all green

Deliverable. A full technical audit in week one (Screaming Frog + manual schema validation + CWV report), with a prioritised fix list ranked by impact. We implement in weeks 2–3 alongside your web team.
