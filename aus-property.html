Technical & On-Page Audit

What's breaking on the site today — and what's costing rankings every month it stays unfixed.

AUDIT FINDINGS

Eight Issues Are Doing Most of the Damage.

Surfaced from a full crawl of auspropertyprofessionals.com.au, its sitemap (139 pages + 376 blog posts), schema markup, robots.txt, and a sample of key service + location pages.

Critical

NAP inconsistency

Schema says "Level 3, 100 Harris St, Pyrmont". Contact page says "Miranda + Double Bay". Google can't reconcile where APP actually operates — which is why local pack presence outside inner Sydney is weak.

Critical

Broken structured data

Current schema includes a malformed VideoObject with a base64-encoded iframe dumped into the transcript field. No RealEstateAgent, no LocalBusiness, no Person for Lloyd. This is the single biggest AI/rich-results blocker.

Critical

Zero FAQPage schema

Every major service page has a visible FAQ block — none are wrapped in FAQPage JSON-LD. Missing the single easiest SERP-feature and AI-citation win on the entire site.

High

H1s aren't targeting keywords

Homepage H1: "REINSW Buyer's Agent Of The Year" (award, not keyword). /home-buyer H1: "Buy A Home" (two words). /investor H1: "Full Comprehensive Property Search". These are all rewritable today for immediate lift.

High

61% of images have empty alt

27 of 44 homepage <img> tags have alt="". Awards, team photos, suburb imagery, logos — all invisible to search and accessibility. Site-wide sweep needed.

High

Duplicate location templates

Melbourne and Brisbane pages are near-identical boilerplate with the city name swapped. Google's duplicate-content heuristics demote templated pages — one of the main reasons APP isn't ranking outside Sydney.

Medium

Dual URL trees for locations

Some location pages live at /locations/, others at /about-us/locations-we-cover/. Splits internal PageRank, confuses crawlers, and leaves orphan URLs (e.g. /buyers-agent-newcastle-old/) in the sitemap.

Medium

WordPress stack bloat

Salient theme + WPBakery (legacy) + WP Rocket + WooCommerce + GTM + GoHighLevel embed. Mobile Core Web Vitals are almost certainly failing. Page speed is a direct ranking factor and a conversion killer.

STRUCTURED DATA

The Schema Is the Biggest Missed Win.

What's on the site today

  • Hand-coded Organization with the wrong address
  • SASWP-generated VideoObject with a base64 iframe inside the transcript field (broken)
  • A near-empty duplicate Organization with blank sameAs
  • No schema on 376 blog posts
  • No schema on any location page
  • No AggregateRating wired to the Good Reviews widget

What we'll implement

  • RealEstateAgent + LocalBusiness at the correct address
  • Person schema for Lloyd, wired to LinkedIn, Instagram, book, podcast
  • Book schema for Positively Geared 1st + 2nd editions
  • PodcastSeries + PodcastEpisode for Positively Geared podcast
  • FAQPage across 10+ service and location pages
  • BreadcrumbList site-wide
  • AggregateRating + Review from Good Reviews
  • Article / BlogPosting on every post
  • Awards surfaced in Organization.award[]

Schema is how a domain says "this is who I am" in a way Google and AI models can read. Lloyd's credentials are invisible to machines right now. Once they're properly structured, every AI model — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews — can cite APP as the expert. That's a compounding asset, not a one-off fix.
