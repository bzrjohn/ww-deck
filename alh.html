The 12% Gap

Only 12% of sources cited by AI engines overlap with Google's top 10 organic results. Being on page 1 of Google does not mean AI cites you. AEO is an entirely separate competitive landscape.

Avg citations per Perplexity answer: 6.61 sources

Avg citations per ChatGPT answer: 2.62 sources

What Wins AI Citations

  • Structured FAQ schema — direct Q&A format AI can extract
  • Specific data tables with numbers, not vague generalities
  • Authoritative author attribution (E-E-A-T signals)
  • Freshness signals — regularly updated content with dates
  • Comprehensive coverage — deep pages that answer follow-up questions
  • Domain authority — high-DR sites dominate AI citations

Strategy: Every new content piece will be structured with FAQ schema, direct-answer formatting, specific Australian data, and attributed authorship. This positions Sleep Solutions Australia to be cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews alongside traditional search rankings.
